## Project Overview

Rinconada Stereo Web — Radio en línea y señal digital cultural de San José de la Rinconada.

## Institutional Identity & Terminology Rules

- **Strict Prohibition**: Never use the terms *"emisora comunitaria"*, *"radio comunitaria"*, or *"de interés social"* in UI copy, comments, metadata, commit messages, or documentation. In Colombia, "emisora comunitaria" is a restricted legal license for FM broadcast concessions issued by MinTIC, which does not apply to this online streaming station.
- **Approved Terminology**: *"Radio en línea"*, *"emisora en vivo"*, *"emisora en directo"*, *"señal digital"*, *"encuentro con tu región"*, *"la voz de la ciénaga"*.
- **Principio de Marketing**: Show, don't tell. Community connection is demonstrated through music, local greetings, and culture—not bureaucratic descriptors.
